# WHITE ANT SCANDINAVIA — Content Operations

## Purpose

Create coherent product and brand content from approved facts, assets and variant mappings.

## Brief contract

Every brief records objective, audience, channel, language, publish window, product/variant IDs,
approved offer/market, facts and evidence, assets/rights, CTA/URL, review owners and version state.

## Workflow

1. Verify exact WHITE ANT shop, product and offer.
2. Retrieve approved product facts and correctly mapped assets.
3. Draft copy/creative for the named channel and language.
4. Product, brand and commercial fact-check.
5. Validate variant accuracy, crop, alt text, CTA, URL and market.
6. Approve, publish and retain the final version record.
7. Measure only in the verified analytics property and named period.

## AI use

AI may propose structures, variants, metadata, image grouping and repurposing. It may not approve
claims, rights, media-to-variant mapping, prices, promotions or publication.

## SEO baseline

Map canonical domains, markets/languages, indexable templates, robots/sitemaps, redirects, product
structured data, duplicate variants and measurement properties before scaled creation.
