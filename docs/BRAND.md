# WHITE ANT SCANDINAVIA — Brand Operations

## Status

WHITE ANT SCANDINAVIA is the clothing brand in the portfolio. No approved brand kit, visual tokens,
audience definition, claims library or tone-of-voice source is present in this repository.
“SCANDINAVIA” in the name is not evidence of product origin, manufacturing location, materials or
sustainability.

## Required source pack

- Purpose, positioning, category and target customers.
- Approved company/brand naming and legal identity.
- Logo files, colour, typography, layout and image rules.
- Voice, vocabulary, language variants and channel examples.
- Product/material/origin/care and sustainability evidence.
- Approved shipping, return, warranty and promotion wording.
- Current approved assets with usage rights and product/variant mapping.

## Claim discipline

Do not publish as fact without evidence:

- Scandinavian/Nordic design, origin or manufacture;
- material composition, craftsmanship or production method;
- sustainable, ethical, timeless, premium or durable claims;
- customer reviews, awards, press or popularity;
- scarcity, limited stock, discount/reference price or delivery time.

## Content states

Draft → product fact-check → brand review → commercial/legal review → approved → superseded.

Every approved asset/copy item records channel, language, products/variants, source, reviewer and
approval date.

## Until mapping is complete

- Reuse only assets and claims verified in the exact live shop or approved library.
- Label creative direction as a proposal.
- Do not derive permanent tokens from screenshots alone.
- When assets conflict, prefer the newest approved version and explicitly flag supersession.
