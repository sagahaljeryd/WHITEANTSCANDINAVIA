# WHITE ANT SCANDINAVIA — Drive Structure

Apply this structure only inside the authenticated WHITE ANT SCANDINAVIA Drive. Never create it in
Saga's private Drive, Clarté's account or another project.

1. `00 Company & Strategy` — goals, business model, plans and approved commercial strategy.
2. `01 Brand` — logos, identity, voice, campaign system and approved assets/rights.
3. `02 Product` — clothing range, SKU/variant packs, material, fit, measurement and care evidence.
4. `03 Finance` — budgets, invoices, payouts and accounting evidence for the exact legal entity.
5. `04 Legal` — terms, privacy, product/marketing claims, contracts and storefront reviews.
6. `05 Marketing & Content` — launches, creators, email, social, SEO and approved product copy.
7. `06 Operations` — Shopify, sourcing, media mapping, fulfilment, support and release evidence.
8. `07 Research` — audience, market, competitor and channel research with dates/sources.
9. `08 Archive` — superseded range, brand, media and campaign material.

Do not store credentials or raw customer/order exports in Drive working folders or Git. Use the exact
WHITE ANT Shopify shop and accounting system for current operational objects.
